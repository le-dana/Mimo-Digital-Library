The Friendly Giraffe's Long Neck

By Ella Sunshine
Gerry the Giraffe learns how his tall neck helps all his forest friends reach high places.

Page 1

Once upon a time, high above the grassy savanna, Gerry the Giraffe poked his nose into the fluffy white clouds.

Page 2

All the small animals looked up in awe. 'Gerry, can you help us reach the sweet apples on top of the big tree?' asked Barnaby the Bunny.

Page 3

Gerry smiled happily, stretched his friendly long neck, and gently picked the juiciest red apple for everyone to share!
